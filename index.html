What we found · checked 21 October 2026

Sterling has plenty of reach. The listings are losing people.

Sterling is already on RentCafe, Zumper, PadMapper, Rentals.ca, Craigslist, Apartments.com and more. The gap isn't more ads. It's what renters see when they land on one. Tap any screenshot to enlarge it.

Listings

Some listings don't say "Sterling"

Zumper calls the building 4118 Cambie St, with the postal code V5Y None. Someone searching for Sterling won't find it there.

Photos

The first image is a text graphic

On Zumper and Craigslist the lead image is a "Studios Available Now" or "2 Months Free" graphic, not the building or a home. On Zumper, the "14 Photos" button sits on top of the phone number.

Craigslist

3 of 4 Craigslist ads have no photos

Both 1-bed ads and one 2-bed ad are text only. Ads with no photos get passed over, even with a strong offer.

Accuracy

Ads carry old or wrong details

  • October ads still say "Available July 1st" and "August 1st".
  • One ad says the effective rent is $414.
  • The 1-bed effective rents don't add up: $3,000 × 12 ÷ 14 is $2,571, but the ad shows $2,371.
  • 2-bath homes are listed as 1 bath.
Website

24 floor plans, no clear path

  • 15 of the 24 plans say "Inquire for details".
  • Most plans have a den. No plan name mentions it.
  • Plans A2 and A2B show each other's drawings.
  • Amenities are a plain text list with no photos.
Tours & offer

The offer gets lost, and tours take effort

  • "2 Months Free" is navy on navy, so it doesn't stand out.
  • The website shows "from $2,100*" but never says what the offer is.
  • There's no online tour booking. Renters have to text a number.
  • There's no virtual tour. The site's tour section is switched off.

What changes

Same building, same systems. A much clearer story.
